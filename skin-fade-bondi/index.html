Questions
How long does a skin fade last?

A fade grows out fast. Most clients come back every two to three weeks to keep the line clean.

Low, mid or high fade?

Low starts just above the ear, mid around the temple, high near the crown. Not sure? Your barber will tell you what suits your head shape.

Can I choose my barber?
